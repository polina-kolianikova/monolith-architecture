# MONOLITH visual direction

The house is represented by coherent photographic imagery generated from one exterior reference. Limestone grain, bronze structure, realistic glazing, oak joinery and the Atlantic landscape replace the former primitive slab model. Newsreader and Archivo retain the established typography.

Each chapter has a deliberate native-flow photograph and reading region. On phones all controls remain in the document and every picture has intrinsic dimensions. Scroll drives only a small bounded pan, without blanking the object, fitting it into arbitrary text gaps or pinning controls over content. Desktop uses alternating editorial grids.

Light mood controls a photographic treatment. View choices refer to actual supplied photographs. They do not promise model orbit or hidden geometry. The architecture and property are fictional concepts.
